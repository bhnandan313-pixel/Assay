# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Assay project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment for all participants.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful** – treat everyone with kindness and respect.
- **Be collaborative** – value constructive feedback and diverse perspectives.
- **Be inclusive** – avoid language or actions that marginalize or exclude.
- **Avoid harassment** – refrain from any form of harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- **Follow community guidelines** – adhere to the project's contribution guidelines and code of conduct.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered harassment and are unacceptable:
- Violence, threats of violence, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory remarks or jokes based on protected characteristics.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Persistent unwanted contact, including stalking or following.
- Publishing private or personal information about others without consent.
- Disruption of the community or project work.

## Enforcement

Instances of harassment or other code of conduct violations may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

If a participant engages in harassing behavior, the maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning the offender or expulsion from the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within the project's public spaces (such as the repository, issue tracker, mailing lists, chat rooms) and in any private interactions related to the project.

## Contact

If you experience or witness any violation of this Code of Conduct, please contact the maintainers at `maintainers@assayproject.org`.
